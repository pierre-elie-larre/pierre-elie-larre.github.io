Welcome!

I am a CNRS researcher at LPTMS (Laboratory of Theoretical Physics and Statistical Models), part of Université Paris-Saclay in Orsay, France. My research focuses on theoretical physics with an emphasis on experimental relevance, specifically:

  • Quantum fluids
    Ultracold atomic gases, exciton-polariton condensates, and paraxial superfluids of light
  • Superfluid transport
    Frictionless flow, critical velocities, and dissipation mechanisms
  • Nonlinear waves
    Solitons, vortices, and shock waves in nonlinear dispersive media
  • Quench dynamics
    Far-from-equilibrium dynamics following sudden parameter changes
  • Analog gravity
    Curved-spacetime phenomena in tabletop laboratory experiments

This website serves as a repository of research and teaching materials for colleagues and students, many of which are not publicly available elsewhere. Take a look around!
