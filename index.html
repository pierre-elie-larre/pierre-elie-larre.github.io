Welcome!

I am a CNRS researcher at LPTMS, part of Université Paris-Saclay in Orsay, France. As a theoretical physicist with a focus on experimental relevance, my research deals with:

  • Quantum fluids: Ultracold atomic gases, exciton-polariton condensates, and paraxial superfluids of light.
  • Superfluid transport: Frictionless flow, critical velocities, and dissipation mechanisms.
  • Nonlinear waves: Solitons, vortices, and shock waves in nonlinear dispersive media.
  • Quench dynamics: Nonequilibrium phenomena following sudden parameter changes.
  • Analog gravity: Simulation of Hawking radiation in tabletop laboratory experiments.
